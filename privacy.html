Right-Size Meetings Study

Privacy

This page explains, in plain language, how information from this research study is handled. Last updated 2 August 2026.

The short version

The study collects anonymous answers. It does not use cookies, advertising, or third-party tracking.

What we collect

We collect your anonymous survey answers, along with basic technical details that arrive with them: your browser type, screen size, language, the page that referred you, and how long each section took.

If you choose to leave an email address at the end, it is stored with your answers so the researcher can follow up — which means that response is no longer anonymous. Leave it blank and your response stays fully anonymous.

The interactive story and the walkthrough may also record rough interaction information, such as which character you played, which choices you made, and which option you picked. This helps the researcher understand how people respond to the fictional concept.

Two survey questions may be tailored to you: your earlier anonymous answers (never your email) are sent to an AI service (Google's Gemini) to write two personalized follow-up questions. The questions and your answers are stored with the rest of your response. If the AI service is unavailable, standard questions are used instead.

Where it goes

Study responses are stored in a private Google Sheet (hosted by Google) that only the researcher can read. They are never sold or shared with advertisers.

What we do not collect

We do not use cookies, ads, or trackers. Browser local storage is used only to help you resume the survey if you leave before finishing it.

How long we keep it

Study information is deleted within 12 months of the study closing, and no later than 31 August 2027.

Your choices and rights

You can email themastergts007@gmail.com to ask for your answers to be removed.

About the walkthrough

The product shown in the walkthrough is a fictional early concept being researched. It is not a finished product for sale.
