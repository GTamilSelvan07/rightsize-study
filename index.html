Price the actual product and value package below — not a meeting recorder or summary tool.

  1. 1
    Product

    An AI-assisted decision workspace for software agencies and consultancies

    When a client asks to change scope, a delivery date, or price, it brings together the permitted project evidence, shows the one trade-off only people can decide, and recommends no meeting, written approval, or the smallest necessary conversation.

  2. 2
    Value

    Reach a clear, accountable decision with less coordination

    • Recover attentionAvoid a broad status meeting when written approval or a short conversation is enough.
    • Protect delivery and marginMake the scope–date–price trade-off clear before anyone makes an accidental promise.
    • Give both sides controlKeep private limits private while the agency and client approve exactly the same terms.
    • Carry the decision throughRecord owners and dates, then show whether the agreed outcome happened.
